# Research and Verification

For every critical claim record claim/parameter, value and units, source/revision, page/section/table, conditions and footnotes, status, and conflicts.

For component research use the exact manufacturer ordering code. Confirm suffixes, package, temperature grade, tolerance, pinout, operating conditions, absolute maximum ratings, thermal data, and errata. Distributor listings support sourcing research but do not replace datasheets.

For standards confirm edition, jurisdiction, product category, and applicability. Do not reproduce copyrighted standards text; summarize and cite.
