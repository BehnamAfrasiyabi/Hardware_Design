# Engineering Principles

1. Requirements before implementation: define measurable behavior and constraints.
2. Evidence before assertion: trace critical claims to sources or calculations.
3. Worst-case before nominal-only: include tolerance, temperature, supply, load, aging, and transients as relevant.
4. Design for failure: identify fault states, safe states, protection, diagnostics, and recovery.
5. Design for test: make critical behavior observable.
6. Design for manufacture: account for assembly, tolerances, inspection, and supply chain.
7. Security by design: consider debug access, boot chain, firmware updates, secrets, and interfaces.
8. Iterative verification: retain failures and corrective actions.
9. Reproducibility: record tool versions, source revisions, constraints, and artifacts.
10. AI recommendations require human review proportional to risk.
