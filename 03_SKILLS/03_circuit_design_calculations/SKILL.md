# Circuit Design and Engineering Calculations
Status: Draft

## Workflow
1. Define function, operating modes, interfaces, and failure conditions.
2. Describe topology and current/voltage paths.
3. Establish equations from suitable device models and authoritative references.
4. Calculate nominal and worst-case behavior: tolerances, temperature, supply, load, startup, transients, and faults.
5. Check stress, dissipation, thermal rise, stability, noise, bandwidth, timing, and margins.
6. Review protection, sequencing, grounding, return paths, and adjacent-block interactions.
7. Independently recalculate critical results or use a second method.
8. Define simulation and bench measurements for validation.

## Output
Topology, equations, assumptions, calculations with units, worst-case results, stress table, risks, verification plan.

## Acceptance
Inputs and equations are traceable; units are consistent; operating and absolute maximum limits are distinguished; margins are explicit.
