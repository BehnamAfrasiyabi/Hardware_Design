# Component Research and Selection
Status: Draft

## Workflow
1. Convert circuit needs into electrical, functional, mechanical, environmental, lifecycle, and sourcing constraints.
2. Identify exact orderable manufacturer part numbers.
3. Obtain current datasheet, errata, reference design, and application notes.
4. Verify pinout, package, operating conditions, absolute maximum ratings, electrical characteristics, timing, thermal data, and derating.
5. Check corner cases and compatibility with surrounding components.
6. Compare alternatives with explicit criteria; identify second sources where practical.
7. Timestamp volatile lifecycle, stock, and lead-time research.
8. Verify footprint against package drawing and land-pattern guidance.

## Output
Comparison table, selected part and rationale, exact ordering code, source citations with page/table, footprint source, risks, alternatives, and open items.

## Acceptance
No critical parameter depends only on AI output or distributor snippets. Datasheet conditions and suffix match the exact part.
