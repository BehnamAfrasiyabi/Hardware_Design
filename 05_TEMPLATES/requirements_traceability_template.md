## Rules
- One independently verifiable requirement per row.
- Record assumptions separately; do not disguise assumptions as approved requirements.
- Use stable IDs and never silently reuse a retired ID.
- Define acceptance criteria before verification.
- Status values: Open, Designed, Verified, Failed, Blocked, Waived (approval reference required).
- A Waived requirement is not Verified.
- Every change records impact on architecture, schematic, PCB, firmware, verification, cost, schedule and compliance.
