# General Hardware Design Review

- [ ] Requirements are measurable and traceable.
- [ ] Interfaces and operating modes are defined.
- [ ] Datasheets, errata, revisions, and exact ordering codes are verified.
- [ ] Recommended operating conditions and absolute maximum ratings are distinguished.
- [ ] Worst-case tolerance, temperature, supply, load, and transient conditions are considered.
- [ ] Power, thermal, startup, shutdown, and fault conditions are reviewed.
- [ ] Protection and safe-state behavior are reviewed.
- [ ] Clock, reset, boot, programming, and debug paths are reviewed.
- [ ] Signal integrity, return paths, grounding, and EMC risks are reviewed.
- [ ] Creepage, clearance, isolation, and applicable safety requirements are reviewed.
- [ ] PCB footprint and mechanical fit are verified.
- [ ] ERC/DRC findings are dispositioned.
- [ ] Test points and production test strategy are defined.
- [ ] BOM lifecycle, sourcing, and approved alternatives are reviewed.
- [ ] Firmware update, debug access, and security risks are reviewed where applicable.
- [ ] Open issues have owners and dispositions.
